# My World FAQ — comprehension and fairness checkpoint

Status: **pending unfamiliar-reader review**

This checkpoint must pass before the prototype is treated as publication-ready
or the production visual/feedback phases are declared complete. It is a small
usability check, not prevalence evidence or a product-validation study.

## Participants

Recruit 3–5 MOE/DXD colleagues who did not author the page, including at least
one person reading with a parent-minded perspective. Do not brief them on the
intended answers before the exercise.

## Review script

Open the exact-link prototype at `/my-world/faq`.

1. After about 20 seconds, ask the reader to explain:
   - the current project stage;
   - Capture → Sensemake → Review → Act/Return;
   - what is implemented versus what a pilot would still test.
2. Ask them to find one concern relevant to them in about 10 seconds.
3. Ask them to open the short answer and then its evidence.
4. Ask them to identify:
   - one Product fact;
   - one Research-backed point and its limitation;
   - one Team check or Open question.
5. Ask whether the concern feels represented fairly, particularly the
   dinner-table, dependency, screen-time, privacy, and unsafe-home concerns.
6. Ask what sounds defensive, dismissive, too certain, or hard to scan.

## Stop conditions

Revise and repeat the checkpoint if any reader interprets the page as formal
approval or proven safety/efficacy, or if most readers cannot:

- explain the stage and four-step loop;
- find a relevant concern quickly;
- distinguish known product behavior from research, field signals, and Team
  checks;
- recognise that individual event/community comments are question-language
  signals rather than representative evidence.

A parent-minded concern that feels minimised or argued away also requires
revision and another review.
